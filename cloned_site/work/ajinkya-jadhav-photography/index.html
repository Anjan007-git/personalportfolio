(01)

Problem

A working photographer in Pune, with more than ten years behind the camera, needed a portfolio that matched the quality of the work, and a way for clients to book studio time without a back-and-forth of messages.

(02)

Approach

A high-end portfolio organised by service, with a full showcase for each, and a studio rental site alongside it. Images lead; the interface stays out of their way. It is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with GSAP for scroll-driven motion and Swiper for the galleries.

(03)

What's on the site

  • A page per service. Weddings, pre-wedding, fashion and portfolios, corporate and commercial, interiors and architecture, and lifestyle, each with its own gallery.
  • Portfolio catalogue. Selected images by category, opening in a full-screen viewer.
  • Studio 41. A separate section for the photographer's rental studio in Pune: the space and its specifications, switchable sets, lighting and grip, talent and styling support, and a booking call to action.
  • Contact. An enquiry page and a floating WhatsApp button on every page, because that is how most clients get in touch.
  • Search. A sitemap, robots file and page-level metadata aimed at local searches for a photographer in Pune.
